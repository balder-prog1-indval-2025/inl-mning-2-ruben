Getting started
You start with one synth already created — dive straight in. Click on a synth to select it; the selected synth is the one you control with your keyboard and edit notes on. Press Add synth when you want another.

Important: every oscillator (including the one in the starting synth) is silent until you draw its waveform. Open the waveform editor on the oscillator, draw a shape on the canvas, preview it, then close — only then will that oscillator produce sound. You must do this for every new oscillator you create.

Each synth has its own row of notes on the timeline. Click a cell to place a note; click further to the right of an existing note to extend its length.

Playback
Set the tempo (BPM) in the toolbar, then press the green play button to play back every synth's timeline together. The red stop button halts playback.

Keyboard controls
With a synth selected, the keyboard acts like a piano (lower row = white keys, upper row = black keys):
A W S E D F T G Y H U J K O L P
covers C4 up to D#5 chromatically.

Timeline navigation
Click the timeline canvas to focus it, then use ← / → to scroll the piano roll left and right. Scrolling is clamped at the start so you can't go past bar 0.

Tips
Make sure a synth is selected (highlighted) before playing, otherwise key presses are silent. Held keys won't stack — repeats are ignored.
